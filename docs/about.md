Guangzhou BST Technology Services Co., Ltd. is a medium-sized quality assurance company based in Guangzhou, China. Our predecessor is Brightsky Technology Services Co., Ltd., founded in Hong Kong. BST has a sound inspection network across 8 Asian countries, including China, Vietnam, India, Bangladesh, Thailand, Malaysia, Cambodia, and Sri Lanka.

BST provides one-stop quality assurance services, from factory audit to loading check on site.

Our inspection work covers three lines: consumer products (appliances, furniture, kitchen items, consumer electronics, lighting, toys, gifts and crafts, hardware), industrial products (wire and cable, steel pipe and tower, structural material, pumps, boilers, valves, oil pipes) and medical products (swabs, bandages, blood pressure monitors, face masks, thermometers). Industrial products are still a small part of our business, and they are where we are putting resources over the next five years.

Our commitments are concrete: short response times, competitive prices, effective technical support, professional customer service, and accurate, timely reports. These commitments help clients improve product quality and build confidence in their brands.
