We reply within 24 hours to start scoping the job, and aim to confirm the order within three days. Inspection dates are normally booked at least three working days ahead; urgent requests are handled subject to availability.

## 1. Submit the booking form

Download the [booking form](/BST%20booking%20form.doc), fill it in and email it to [jeffrey@bstinspection.com](mailto:jeffrey@bstinspection.com).

## 2. Send the inspection documents

- Purchase order copy
- Full packing approval sample
- Customer requirements or specifications (if any)
- Proforma invoice (if any)
- Packing list (if any)
- Supplier contact details
- Factory contact details and map

## 3. Inspectors carry out the inspection

BST inspectors work to the inspection specification approved by the client, or to BST's general inspection scope. A draft report is issued at the end of the inspection.

## 4. Receive the report on the next working day

The final report is issued on the next working day after the inspection.
