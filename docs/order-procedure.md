## 1. First: Submit inspection application form.

Please download a [Booking Form](/BST%20booking%20form.doc) and fill in relevant information then send it back to us by email ([info@bstinspection.com](mailto:info@bstinspection.com))

## 2. Second: Provide inspection information:

- Purchase order copy
- Full packing approval sample
- Customer require or specification (If any)
- Proforma invoice (If any)
- Packing list (If any)
- Suppliers' contact information
- Factory's contact information and map

## 3. Third: Inspectors perform the inspection at your factory.

The inspection will be conducted by BST inspectors according to client's approval inspection specification or BST general inspection scopes. Draft inspection reports will be issued at the end of inspection.

## 4. Fourth: Issuing inspection report on the next workday of inspection date.

BST inspection can be able to perform quality inspections and factory audits within a minimum notice of 24 hours.
