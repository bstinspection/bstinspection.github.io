Our prices are competitive because we keep our internal costs in check and pass the savings on to clients. We won't undercut with prices we can't sustain, and we won't cut service standards to win business.

Inspection Services: USD198 per Man-Day; Factory Audit Services: USD258 per Man-Day.

Inspection charges are quoted on a man-day basis. One man-day is one inspector working a day (8 hours). The number of man-days depends on product complexity, shipment quantity, inspection location, and specification requirements. Additional expenses such as travel and accommodation are charged at actual cost.

For an exact quotation, tell us what you are producing, how many, and where the factory is.
