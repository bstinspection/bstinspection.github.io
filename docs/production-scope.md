## Current Product Scope

### 1. Consumer Products

a. Electric appliances and commodities: household and similar electrical appliances, small domestic appliances, daily commodities.

b. Furniture and kitchen items: household and office furniture, cookware, tableware, kitchen utensils.

c. Consumer electronics and lighting products: computer components, audio and video accessories, lamps, Christmas light chains.

d. Toys, gifts and crafts: toys, giftware, promotional products, porcelain, handicrafts.

e. Hardware and sundries: machinery, tools, stainless tools, stainless kitchenware, general merchandise, sports goods.

f. Garments and shoes.

### 2. Industrial Products

a. Supplier survey.

b. Witnessing of industrial equipment.

c. Product inspection: wire and cable, steel pipe and tower, structural materials, pumps, boilers, valves, oil pipes.

d. Steel structure, crane, ship, oil and gas inspection against international standards.

### 3. Medical Products

a. Swabs, bandages, medical tape, eye pads.

b. Blood pressure monitors, finger pulse oximeters, thermometers.

c. Face masks and other personal protective supplies.

## Our Focus Over the Next Five Years

### Industrial Products

Industrial products account for a limited share of our business today. Over the next five years, resources will be directed to this business line, with the goal of establishing it as one of our core businesses.

Categories we are expanding:

- Wire and cable
- Steel pipe and tower
- Structural materials
- Pumps, boilers, valves
- Oil pipes

These categories follow the same steps as our existing work: supplier survey before the order is confirmed, witnessing of equipment during production, and inspection against international standards before shipment. Our inspectors in China and the other seven Asian countries we cover are all able to take on these assignments.
