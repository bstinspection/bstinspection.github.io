Brightsky Technology Services Co., Ltd. (Hong Kong) and Guangzhou BST Technology Services Co., Ltd. have officially merged, forming a unified and more powerful quality assurance service platform.

**What This Means for Our Clients**

The merger combines Brightsky's international client network and Hong Kong-based operational expertise with Guangzhou BST's strong on-the-ground inspection team and deep knowledge of manufacturing practices across China. Together, we now offer:

- **Broader geographic coverage** — seamless service from factory audits in southern China to loading supervision in northern ports.
- **Streamlined operations** — a single point of contact for all quality assurance needs, from initial production checks to final random inspection.
- **Consistent service standards** — unified inspection procedures, reporting formats, and quality benchmarks across all service lines.

**Looking Forward**

This merger marks a significant milestone in BST's growth. Our combined team is better equipped than ever to help importers, retailers, and distributors reduce supply chain risk and ensure product quality. We remain committed to the principles of justice, integrity, and science that have always defined our work.
