Brightsky Technology Services Co., Ltd. (Hong Kong) and Guangzhou BST Technology Services Co., Ltd. have merged into one quality assurance service platform.

## What This Means for Our Clients

The merger combines Brightsky's international client network and Hong Kong operational experience with Guangzhou BST's on-the-ground inspection team and its knowledge of manufacturing in China. Now we can:

- Cover more ground: from factory audits in southern China to loading supervision at northern ports.
- Streamline operations: one contact point for all quality assurance needs, from initial production checks to final random inspection.
- Keep standards consistent: uniform inspection procedures, reporting formats, and quality benchmarks across all service lines.

## Looking Forward

The merger gives us a fuller team and a better position to help importers, retailers, and distributors control supply chain risk and keep product quality in check. The principles of fairness, integrity, and science have guided us since day one, and they still do.
