Brightsky Technology Services Co., Ltd. (Hong Kong) and Guangzhou BST Technology Services Co., Ltd. have completed a merger, forming a unified quality assurance service platform.

**What This Means for Our Clients**

The merger combines Brightsky's international client network and Hong Kong operational experience with Guangzhou BST's on-the-ground inspection team and its in-depth knowledge of manufacturing in China. Now we can:

- Broader geographic coverage: from factory audits in southern China to loading supervision at northern ports.
- Streamlined operations: all quality assurance needs are handled through a single point of contact, from initial production checks to final random inspection.
- Consistent service standards: uniform inspection procedures, reporting formats, and quality benchmarks across all service lines.

**Looking Forward**

The merger gives us a more complete team and a stronger position to help importers, retailers, and distributors reduce supply chain risk and ensure product quality. The principles of fairness, integrity, and science have guided BST since its founding.
