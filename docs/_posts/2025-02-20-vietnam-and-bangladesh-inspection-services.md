BST has expanded its service coverage to Vietnam and Bangladesh, two of Asia's fastest-growing manufacturing hubs. Clients who want to move supply beyond China now have more options.

## Services Now Available in Vietnam and Bangladesh

Full services can now be booked in both countries:

- Factory Audit (FA): assess manufacturer capabilities, quality systems, and production capacity.
- Social Compliance Audit (SCA): evaluate working conditions, labor practices, and code of conduct compliance.
- Initial Production Check (IPC): verify raw materials and components before mass production begins.
- During Production Check (DUPRO): monitor quality at 20–30% production completion.
- Final Random Inspection (FRI): pre-shipment inspection of finished goods.
- Loading Supervision (L/S): oversee container loading and sealing.

## Key Manufacturing Regions Covered

In Vietnam, we serve clients in Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Binh Duong, and Dong Nai. In Bangladesh, we cover Dhaka, Chittagong, and Gazipur, the country's main industrial zones.

## What This Means for Our Clients

Clients benefit from:
- One QA partner across several countries, which keeps coordination costs down.
- The same inspection standards and reporting format everywhere.
- Local knowledge: our inspectors are native speakers familiar with local manufacturing practices and regulations.
