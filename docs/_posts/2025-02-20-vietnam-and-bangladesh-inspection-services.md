BST has expanded our service coverage to Vietnam and Bangladesh, two of Asia's fastest-growing manufacturing hubs. This strategic expansion allows us to support clients who are diversifying their supply chains beyond China.

**Services Now Available in Vietnam and Bangladesh**

Our full range of quality assurance services is now available in both countries:

- **Factory Audit (FA)** — assess manufacturer capabilities, quality systems, and production capacity.
- **Social Compliance Audit (SCA)** — evaluate working conditions, labor practices, and code of conduct compliance.
- **Initial Production Check (IPC)** — verify raw materials and components before mass production begins.
- **During Production Check (DUPRO)** — monitor quality at 20–30% production completion.
- **Final Random Inspection (FRI)** — pre-shipment inspection of finished goods.
- **Loading Supervision (L/S)** — oversee container loading and sealing.

**Key Manufacturing Regions Covered**

In Vietnam, our inspectors serve clients in Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Binh Duong, and Dong Nai. In Bangladesh, we cover Dhaka, Chittagong, and Gazipur — the country's primary industrial zones.

**Why This Matters for Our Clients**

By extending our service network, BST offers true end-to-end supply chain quality assurance across Asia's three largest sourcing destinations. Clients benefit from:
- Reduced coordination costs with a single QA partner across multiple countries.
- Consistent inspection standards and reporting across all locations.
- Local expertise — our inspectors are native speakers familiar with local manufacturing practices and regulations.
