## 2. Provision of services

As an independent third party, BST supplies information in the form of findings or recommendations, to help prevent the risks to which clients are exposed and to help them assure the quality of their products. BST's services (hereinafter called the "services") consist of work performed by BST, including but not limited to:

- Factory Audit (FA)
- Social Compliance Audit (SCA)
- Initial Production Check (IPC)
- In-Process Quality Check (IPQC)
- During Production Check (DUPRO)
- Final Random Inspection (FRI)
- Loading Supervision (L/S)
- Testing Arrangement (TA)

## 6. Liability and Indemnification

### 6.1 Limitation of liability

No action or inspection by BST relieves the manufacturer of its obligation to deliver quality goods, or of compensating the client for poor products. Inspections are simply a set of quality tools the buyer can use to exercise more control over purchasing and reduce errors, misunderstandings, negligence or even fraud. If it is proven that BST staff failed to notice documented defects or safety issues during an inspection, BST will refund the inspection fee — and, in cases of proven extreme willful misconduct, up to ten times that fee. Any concerns about the performance of BST staff should be raised with BST immediately, so that BST can open a claim investigation: checking reference samples, shipment samples and all inspection paperwork to establish where things went wrong.

BST advice is given only in relation to documents and information provided by the client, and BST cannot be held liable if it has received incomplete or false information.
